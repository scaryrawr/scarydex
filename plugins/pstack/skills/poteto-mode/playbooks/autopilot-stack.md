### Autopilot-stack

**You own the stack, never the landing. Hand the operator one linear chain of verified PRs to review and land.** Follow **Autopilot-full** except where this playbook differs. Re-read both at each checkpoint. This one wins on stack ownership and delivery.

1. **Run owners without landing or topology authority.** Owners report STACK-READY with exact head and receipts instead of merge-ready. They never rebase onto another branch, including trunk, retarget, merge, close a PR or arm auto-merge. A dependent item branches from its dependency's branch, not trunk. Owners retain the durable trail, checkpoint, verification and stop contracts from Autopilot-full.
2. **Give topology one writer.** Owners push only their own branches and report each tip, current base and intended parent. The coordinator alone rebases or retargets links. Append only clean independently verified PRs in dependency order. Set the child's base to its parent's branch with `gh pr edit <pr> --base <parent-branch>`. Only the root targets trunk. Graphite is not required. A topology push still needs authorization, exclusive branch ownership and `--force-with-lease` pinned to an independently verified remote SHA. An owner accepts that authorized push as its new base and lease. On conflicts, the owner prepares its scoped resolution for the topology writer to publish, without concurrent writers.
3. **Absorb drift at the root and reverify what moved.** Before landing starts, fetch current trunk and rebase the chain bottom-up when needed. Apply Autopilot-full's exact base/head receipt audit and Shipping's patch-id rule to each link, whose base is its parent. Re-run mergeability and CI after rewritten pushes. A changed parent/base also requires fresh combined-tree integration, live and perf proof; patch equality never waives that requirement. The fresh countersign rule for loosened gates remains unchanged.
4. **Deliver the verified chain, then keep hands off it.** Each link carries its verdict in the PR body or an authorized comment. The operator reviews and lands the chain. No owner merges, closes or arms auto-merge. Once landing starts, only Shipping's coordinated next-link move is allowed under explicit authority. A mid-landing problem reaches the operator and any fix waits for their go. Honor state-then-wait and deliver zero-writes holds immediately.

Use available completion notifications or a bounded heartbeat rather than an assumed recurring host loop. Preserve the objective, child state, trails and receipts in durable local artifacts. Audit until no delegated work remains. When an audit changes something for the operator, use **ketchup**; otherwise stay quiet.

**Choosing between autopilots.** Autopilot-full fits independent PRs with explicit landing authority. Autopilot-stack fits coupled work, operator review before landing, or withheld merge authority.

**Reply:** use **ketchup**, with real root and tip links, each link's verdict, operator gates, and anything parked or excluded with its reason.
